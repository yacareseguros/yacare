# PostSocial
The 1080×1350 feed post: a photo or colour block above, a green zigzag band below carrying the headline, the logo bottom-right.

**Provide**: one photo (warm, real moment of care) or a ground colour, the headline copy, and any partner marks.

- Layouts seen in the approved posts: (1) photo top, `rodape`/`base` zigzag in `verde-yacare` below with the Titulo headline bottom-left and `logo-negativo` bottom-right; (2) `verde-medio` top with the Assinatura lockup over `verde-profundo` with a zigzag join and partner marks bottom-left; (3) photo inside a rounded or square frame from Templates on `off-white` or `verde-yacare`, the jacaré resting on the frame.
- Use the Templates SVGs for the zigzag and frames; never redraw them.
- Margins: text at `margem-post` (112px) from the left and about the same from the bottom.
- Partner marks (Parceiros) sit bottom-left, smaller than the logo.
- Don't place text across the zigzag edge, add gradients or shadows, or use more than one headline per post.
