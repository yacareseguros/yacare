# Titulo
The post headline: Fraunces Light in short stacked lines with one or two key words in Bold Italic.

**Provide**: the copy, already broken into 3–4 lines, and which words to emphasise (wrap them in `<em>`).

- Set lines in `titulo` (84/76px on a 1080 post) and emphasis in `titulo-enfase`. Left-aligned, ragged, starting at `margem-post`.
- Ink: `texto-marca` on `off-white`; `texto-sobre-marca` (white) on `verde-yacare`, `verde-medio`, `verde-profundo` or a photo.
- Do: put the person and the stake in emphasis ("você", "finanças").
- Don't: emphasise more than two words, centre the block, use all caps, or set it smaller than 48px on a 1080 canvas (white on `verde-yacare` needs headline size).
