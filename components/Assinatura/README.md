# Assinatura
The signature lockup: the logo beside a three-line Quicksand tagline, used at the top of a post or as its closing frame.

**Provide**: the tagline in three short lines (default "Proteção / que atravessa / o tempo") and the ground.

- First line `chamada-forte`, following lines `chamada`, ink matching the logo version.
- `logo-preferencial` with `verde-medio` text on `off-white`; `logo-negativo` with white text on `verde-medio`, `verde-yacare` or `verde-profundo`.
- The tagline's cap height aligns with the wordmark; gap between logo and text about one "x".
- Don't add a fourth line, change the typeface, or separate the lockup with a rule.
